Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

MedScore Academy ("the App", "we", "us") is an educational clinical-reasoning game provided by MedScore Enterprise Ltd (Company No. 14340753), a company registered in England and Wales. This policy explains what data the App handles and how.

1. Information we collect

The App is designed to work without accounts. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information from users. Specifically:

  • We do not require sign-in or registration.
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, or contact details through the App.
  • We do not upload your responses, guesses, or game history to any server.

2. Voice input

When you tap the microphone button to speak a diagnosis, the App uses your device's built-in speech recognition (the Web Speech API in your browser, or the Android speech recognizer in the app). Audio is processed by your device or its operating system provider (typically Google) and not stored, transmitted, or accessed by MedScore Academy. Please refer to your device or browser provider's privacy policy for details of how they handle speech data.

3. Text-to-speech

The "Play case" feature uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine to read case clues aloud. No audio is sent to us.

4. Analytics and cookies

The App does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics.

5. Data hosted by third parties

The App is hosted on Vercel Inc. Vercel may collect standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and request timestamps) for the purposes of security and service delivery. See Vercel's Privacy Policy for details.

6. Children

The App is intended for medical students, clinicians, and adult learners. It is not directed at children under 13.

7. Medical disclaimer

MedScore Academy is an educational tool for practising clinical pattern recognition. It is not a diagnostic aid, medical device, or substitute for professional clinical judgement. The content must not be used for real patient care.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of the page will change accordingly.
